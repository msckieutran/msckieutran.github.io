01 / ABOUT

A pharmacist who writes for regulators, clinicians and students.

I am a pharmacist (BPharm; MSc Biomedical Science, University of Montpellier, France) with more than ten years in pharmaceutical science. For over five years I authored CTD-format drug registration dossiers for the Drug Administration of Vietnam (DAV), turning scientific literature and technical data into accurate, compliant documents and resolving regulatory queries within deadlines.

My background spans literature evaluation, clinical pharmacy (including antimicrobial stewardship), teaching and hands-on editing for scientific accuracy, consistency and clarity. I am now seeking a medical writer role at a pharmaceutical company, CRO or medical communications agency, contributing to regulatory and clinical documents.
